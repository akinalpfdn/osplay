### iPad description (1211/4000 chars)

```
Osplay turns this iPad into a real extra monitor for a Mac or a Windows PC. The computer sees a new display: arrange it, drag windows onto it and set its resolution, as with a plugged-in monitor.

Osplay runs on both devices. Install it on your Mac (macOS 14 or later) or Windows PC (Windows 10 or 11) from github.com/akinalpfdn/osplay. It is free and open source.

HOW IT WORKS
- On the computer, choose Extend this Mac or Extend this PC.
- On the iPad, tap the computer under Nearby computers.
- The first time, confirm the same 6-digit code on both screens.

WHAT YOU GET
- A real display, not a mirror: the computer extends onto the iPad.
- Over your Wi-Fi, or over a USB cable for the lowest delay.
- The computer's sound can play on the iPad; the computer's volume keys still work.
- Turn the iPad and the display follows.
- Hardware video decoding for a sharp, smooth picture.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no cloud, no tracking. The picture goes directly from your computer to your iPad over your own network or cable, end-to-end encrypted. Osplay collects no data.

Osplay is a display only: it shows the computer's screen and plays its sound. Touch and pencil input are not sent back to the computer.
```

## Review notes

```
Osplay shows the screen of a Mac or Windows PC that runs Osplay too; it does nothing on its own.

To review:
1. On a Mac (macOS 14 or later), install Osplay from https://github.com/akinalpfdn/osplay/releases/latest/download/Osplay.dmg and choose Extend this Mac in its menu bar menu.
2. Put the Mac and the iPad (or Apple TV) on the same Wi-Fi network. Open Osplay on the iPad and allow Local Network access.
3. Tap the Mac under Nearby computers and confirm the same 6-digit code on both screens.
The Mac now has an extra display shown on the iPad. To hear the Mac's sound there, press the speaker button next to the iPad in the Mac's menu.

Video of these steps: <link>

The app collects no data and needs no account. Local Network access is used to find the computer (Bonjour) and to receive the encrypted stream.
```
